Concluding Summary
The CS120 Survival Guide campaign was created by current students to support future CS120 students by sharing practical advice, lessons learned, and effective strategies for success. Each video focuses on a different aspect of the course, including expectations, available resources, responsible AI use, productive struggle, teaching methodology, collaboration, and effective learning habits.
Although each presenter covered a unique topic, together these videos demonstrate that success in CS120 comes from actively engaging with course resources, communicating with classmates, binstructor, practicing problem-solving skills, attention to detail, and maintaining a growth mindset. By combining our experiences into one educational campaign, we hope future students feel more prepared, confident, and supported throughout the course.
